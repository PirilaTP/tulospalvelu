# 7.2 Did-not-start entries and check markings

### 7.2 Did-not-start entries and check markings

The marking *Did not start* is recorded
per leg, so that the data can be used for tracking those still in the forest
also in cases where an earlier leg is disqualified, and when
orienteers of disqualified teams are also allowed to take part.

The markings are most easily made with the selection *Check and
tracking codes.* In this selection you can also choose that the 1st leg marking is copied automatically
