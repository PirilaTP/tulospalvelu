#### 2.2.3 Defining the distance and technique in a cross-country skiing competition

For the results to be transferred correctly to the systems of the Finnish Ski
Association, the distance and technique must be defined in the class data. The Ski Association
requires the distance to be given in kilometres with one
decimal and the technique with the letter P (classic) or V (free). When the race
includes both techniques, the technique is written as  PV or VP.

The program's earlier way of writing the distance and technique
in the same field still works, but the recommended procedure is to write
the technique in the field reserved for it.
