#### 2.2.3 Defining distance and technique in a skiing competition

For the results to be transferred correctly into the systems of the Finnish Ski Association, the distance
and technique must be defined in the class data field *Distance*, giving both
the distance in kilometres with one decimal and the technique with the letter P (classic) or V (free) immediately
after the distance. When the race includes both techniques, the
letter codes are written in the order in which they are skied. Examples of the correct notation
are 5.0P, 15.0V and 30.0PV.
